Brand Story

Developing consumer electronics since 1951 in Japan.

AIWA has been developing products to meet the growing demands from consumers since 1951 in Japan. The commitment to designing and manufacturing cost effective, high-quality consumer electronics accompanied with world class after-sales service connects the brand with dedicated consumers throughout the regions.

Timeline

The AIWA story in key milestones

1951

Founded & Independent

AIWA begins developing consumer electronics in Japan.

1982

Acquired by Sony Corporation

AIWA joins Sony Corporation as the brand expands its consumer electronics presence.

2006

Brand Discontinued

AIWA brand operations are discontinued after a period of market transition.

2017

AIWA Re-launch & Taiwan Office Established

Independent brand operations resume with Taiwan established as an international coordination base.

2020

Global Business Network Formation

AIWA begins building a broader network of international market and business partners.
