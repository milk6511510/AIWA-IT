Brand History

From Japanese consumer electronics heritage to Taiwan-led international operations.

AIWA has been developing products to meet changing consumer demands since 1951 in Japan. Today, AIWA Electronics International Co., Ltd. presents the brand through international licensing, partner cooperation, and quality governance.

1951

Founded in Japan

AIWA began developing products to meet the growing demands from consumers in Japan.

Consumer electronics era

Product development and regional reach

The brand grew through cost effective, high-quality consumer electronics supported by after-sales service.

International network

Business partnerships across 100+ countries

Distribution, marketing, and retail cooperation expanded across different cultural and market conditions.

Today

Taiwan-led operations

AIWA Electronics International Co., Ltd. coordinates brand licensing, OEM/ODM cooperation, and product approval for markets outside Japan and Korea.
